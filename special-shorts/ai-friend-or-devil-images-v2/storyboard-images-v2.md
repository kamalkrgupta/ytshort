60 seconds, 1080×1920, 30 fps. User requested engaging images instead of a text presentation.

Eight generated photographic illustrations, a recurring fictional adult character and ordinary real-world actions. No claim of documentary footage. Approved synthetic voice clips and timing are unchanged from version 1.

Image generation: built-in imagegen. Prompts and edit history: assets/image-prompts.json. Final consumed assets: public/images/01-hook.png through 08-choice.png (07-control.png is the corrected seatbelt version).
